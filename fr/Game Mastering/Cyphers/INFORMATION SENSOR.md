INFORMATION SENSOR #Cypher #Manifest #Core

Level: 1d6 + 2

Effect: Over the course of one day, the user can activate the cypher a total
number of times equal to its level. Each time, they can select a living creature
within long range and learn the following about it: level, origin, species,
name, and possibly other facts (such as an individual’s credit score, home
address, phone number, and related information).
