MIND-RESTRICTING WALL #Cypher #Fantastic #Core

Level: 1d6 + 2

Effect: Creates an immobile plane of permeable energy up to 20 feet by 20 feet
(6 m by 6 m) for one hour. The plane conforms to the space available.
Intelligent creatures passing through the plane fall unconscious for up to one
hour, or until slapped awake or damaged.
