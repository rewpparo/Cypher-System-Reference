FORCE CUBE #Cypher #Fantastic #Core

Level: 1d6 + 3

Effect: Creates an immobile cube composed of six planes of solid force, each 30
feet (9 m) to a side, for one hour. The planes conform to the space available.
(Although a force cube’s walls are not gaseous permeable, there is likely enough
air within for trapped creatures to breathe for the hour it lasts.)
