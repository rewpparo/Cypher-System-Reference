The Broker type is a cyberpunk variant of the
[Speaker](../../../Characters/Types/Speaker.md) without the overtly supernatural
abilities. Fixers, journalists, media stars, corpos, and public relations
specialists are usually Brokers.

# Broker Stat Pools

Stat Pool Starting Value
- Might 8
- Speed 9
- Intellect 11 You get 6 additional points to divide among your stat pools
  however you wish.

# First-Tier Broker

First-tier Brokers have the following abilities:
- Effort: Your Effort is 1.
- Clever: You have an Intellect Edge of 1, a Might Edge of 0, and a Speed Edge
  of 0.
- Cypher Use: You can bear two cyphers at a time.
- Weapons: You are practiced with light weapons, light firearms, and medium
  firearms without penalty. You have an inability with other medium weapons and
  all heavy weapons; your attacks with medium weapons (other than firearms) and
  heavy weapons are hindered.
- Starting Equipment: Appropriate clothing, a communicator implant, and $5,200.
