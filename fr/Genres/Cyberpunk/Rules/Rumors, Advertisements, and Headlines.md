In an urban setting, characters are surrounded by neon signs and news displays,
inundated with advertisements and rumors—all of which can add flavor to
descriptions of locations, lead the PCs to new encounters, or spark a new
adventure. These tables provide ideas ranging in tone from lighthearted to
dramatic.
