Surveillance Overwatch (3 Intellect points) #Ability #Cyberpunk

When you use your Camera Sight ability to assess a situation and warn another
character of an impending threat, that character’s next defense roll against the
threat you’ve spotted is eased. Likewise, if you use Camera Sight to advise a
character of a foe’s weakness, that character’s next attack roll against that
foe is eased.

Action to initiate.
