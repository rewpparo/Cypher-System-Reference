Heavy Integrated Weapon #Ability #Cyberpunk

You must have at least one gorilla arm to select this ability. One heavy melee
weapon of your choice (such as a chainsaw or omega bat) is built into your
gorilla arm. The weapon is concealed until you wish to use it.

If you have both [Integrated Weapon](Integrated%20Weapon.md) and Heavy
Integrated Weapon, each weapon is built into a different arm.

Enabler.
