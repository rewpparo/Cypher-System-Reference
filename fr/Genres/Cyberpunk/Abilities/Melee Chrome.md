Melee Chrome #Ability #Cyberpunk

Your machine parts grant you +1 to Armor, +1 to your Might Pool, and +1 to your
Speed Pool.

Melee Chrome is a variant of [Enhanced
Body](../../../Characters/Abilities/Enhanced%20Body.md) for a setting where
cybernetics are common and have limited self-healing capabilities; it doesn’t
grant as many Pool points as Enhanced Body, but doesn’t have the limited healing
drawbacks.

Enabler.
