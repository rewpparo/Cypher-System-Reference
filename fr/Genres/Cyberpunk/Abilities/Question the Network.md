Question the Network (5 Intellect points) #Ability #Cyberpunk

You send a digital query to any or all machines within 1 mile (1.5 km).
Depending on the circumstances, this might be a wireless communication or
transmitted through a hard data connection. You can ask one basic question about
the machines or anything happening near them and receive a simple answer. For
example, while in an area with many machines, you could ask about the location
of a specific creature or individual, and if they are within a mile of you, one
or more machines will probably provide the answer.
