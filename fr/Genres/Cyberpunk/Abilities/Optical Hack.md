Optical Hack (2+ Intellect points) #Ability #Cyberpunk

You reboot the technological optical sensors of a target within short range
(such as a robot, surveillance camera, or person with cybernetic optics). If
your Intellect-based attack against them succeeds, the target is blinded for one
round. In addition to the normal options for using Effort, you can choose to use
Effort to increase the range (long for one level of Effort, very long for two,
or 1000 feet (300 m) for three).

A blinded foe acts as if they’re in total darkness; if the attacker has a sense
of where the opponent might be (such as hearing them speak), their attack is
only hindered by four steps, otherwise the attack automatically fails.
