Alchemist Bag #Artifact #Fantasy

Level: 1d6

Form: Embroidered velvet bag

Effect: This bag can contain up to one cypher per artifact level, as long as
each is no larger than a typical potion bottle or scroll case. These cyphers do
not count against a character’s cypher limit.

Depletion: 1 in 1d20 (check each time a cypher is added to the bag)
