Cloak of Finery #Artifact #Fantasy

Level: 1d6 + 1

Form: Multilayered cloak of glittering material

Effect: This cloak is woven of beautiful fibers and set with dazzling gems. It
automatically fits itself to its wearer in the most flattering way. When
activated, it enhances the wearer’s appearance, voice, tone, and even their
grammar, granting an asset to all interaction tasks for the next minute.

Depletion: 1 in 1d20
