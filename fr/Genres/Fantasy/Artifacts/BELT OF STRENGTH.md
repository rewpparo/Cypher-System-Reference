Belt of Strength #Artifact #Fantasy

Level: 1d6

Form: Thick leather belt with a metal buckle and rivets

Effect: The belt enhances the strength and endurance of the wearer. This
increases the wearer’s maximum Might Pool by 5 (or by 7 if the artifact is level
6 or higher). If the wearer removes the belt, any excess Might points above
their normal maximum Might Pool are lost; if they wear the belt again, the
points do not automatically return (they must be restored with recovery rolls,
healing magic, or similar effects).

Depletion: —
