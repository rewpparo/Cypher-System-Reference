Cloak of Balakar #Artifact #Fantasy

Level: 1d6 + 3

Form: Blue cloak with elaborate designs suggesting blowing wind

Effect: The wearer can calm winds of the artifact’s level or lower in a radius
of 1 mile (1.5 km). Up to once a day, the wearer can create a destructive
windstorm up to that size, lasting one minute; this storm’s level is equal to
half the artifact’s level.

Depletion: 1 in 1d6 (on depletion, cloak disappears and reappears somewhere else
in the world)
