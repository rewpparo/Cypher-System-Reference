Necromantic Wand #Artifact #Fantasy

Level: 1d6 + 4

Form: Bone wand carved with runes

Effect: This wand emits a faint short-range beam of sickly violet light that
affects only organic creatures and materials. Living targets hit by the beam
move one step down the damage track. Nonliving organic targets are likely
destroyed.

This device is a rapid-fire weapon and thus can be used with the Spray or Arc
Spray abilities that some characters have, but each “round of ammo” used or each
additional target selected requires an additional depletion roll.

Depletion: 1 in 1d10
