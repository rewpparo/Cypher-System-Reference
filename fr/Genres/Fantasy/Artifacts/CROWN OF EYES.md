Crown of Eyes #Artifact #Fantasy

Level: 1d6

Form: Metallic circlet set with several crystal spheres

Effect: It takes one round to activate the crown. When activated, the crystal
spheres separate from the crown and fly around the wearer at immediate range for
an hour. The wearer can see anything the crystal spheres can see. This allows
the wearer to peek around corners without being exposed to danger. This gives
the wearer an asset in initiative and all perception tasks.

Depletion: 1 in 1d100
