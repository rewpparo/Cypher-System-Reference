Instant Ladder #Artifact #Fantasy

Level: 1d6

Form: Small lightweight metal rod with gem buttons

Effect: When activated, the rod extends and produces rungs so that it can be
used as a ladder up to 28 feet (9 m) long. The ladder can be transformed back
into its rod form from either end.

Depletion: 1 in 1d100

A creature unfamiliar with the buttons on an instant ladder needs to spend
several rounds figuring out the proper sequence to expand or collapse it.
