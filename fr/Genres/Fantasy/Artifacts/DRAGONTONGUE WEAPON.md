Dragontongue Weapon #Artifact #Fantasy

Level: 1d6 + 2

Form: Weapon that roars with red flame when activated, trailing a stream of
black smoke

Effect: This weapon functions as a normal weapon of its type. If the wielder
uses it to attack a foe, upon a successful hit, the wielder decides whether to
activate the flame. Upon activation, the weapon lashes the target with fire,
inflicting additional points of damage equal to the artifact level. The effect
lasts for one minute after each activation.

Depletion: 1 in 1d100
