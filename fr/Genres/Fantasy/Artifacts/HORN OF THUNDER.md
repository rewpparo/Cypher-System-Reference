Horn of Thunder #Artifact #Fantasy

Level: 1d6 + 4

Form: Large signal horn banded with metal and carved with runes

Effect: This massive instrument can barely be held or carried by a single
person. When activated, it emits a 50-foot (15 m) wide cone of pure sonic force
out to long range. Any creature in that area is knocked prone and stunned for
one round, losing its action. Unfixed items the size of a human or smaller are
toppled and/or moved at least 5 feet (1.5 m). Larger objects might also be
toppled.

Depletion: 1 in 1d10
