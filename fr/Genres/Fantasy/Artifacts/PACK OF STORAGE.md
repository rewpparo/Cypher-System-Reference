Pack of Storage #Artifact #Fantasy

Level: 1d6 + 1

Form: Leather backpack or haversack with multiple pockets

Effect: This pack’s mouth can be loosened to open as wide as 6 feet (2 m) in
diameter. It is larger on the inside than on the outside, and can carry up to
500 pounds (226 kg) or 10 cubic feet (.3 cubic m). The pack weighs about
one-tenth as much as it is holding.

Depletion: 1 in 1d100 (check each time something is added to the pack; on
depletion, all objects are expelled from the pack)
