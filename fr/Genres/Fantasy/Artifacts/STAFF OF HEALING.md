Staff of Healing #Artifact #Fantasy

Level: 1d6 + 4

Form: Wooden staff capped with a golden icon

Effect: The staff emits a short-range beam of silvery light that affects only
living creatures. A living creature hit by the beam moves up one step on the
damage track. A target that is not down on the damage track can immediately make
a free recovery roll (or, for NPCs, regain a number of points of health equal to
three times their level).

Depletion: 1 in 1d10
