Stands Like a Bastion #Focus #TankFocus #Core

Your armor, along with your size, strength, incredible training, or machine
enhancement, makes you difficult to move or hurt.

(Some characters who Stand Like a Bastion might already be experts in armor.
They can choose a different tier 1 ability instead of Practiced in Armor.)

- Tier 3: [Unmovable](../Abilities/Unmovable.md)
