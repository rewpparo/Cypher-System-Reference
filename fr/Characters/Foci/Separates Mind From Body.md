Separates Mind From Body #Focus #ExplorationFocus #Core

You can project your mind out of your body to see faraway places and learn
secrets that would otherwise remain hidden.

- Tier 1: [Third Eye](../Abilities/Third%20Eye.md)
- Tier 2: [Open Mind](../Abilities/Open%20Mind.md)
- Tier 2: [Sharp Senses](../Abilities/Sharp%20Senses.md)
- Tier 3: [Roaming Third Eye](../Abilities/Roaming%20Third%20Eye.md) or [Find
  the Hidden](../Abilities/Find%20the%20Hidden.md)
- Tier 4: [Sensor](../Abilities/Sensor.md)
- Tier 5: [Psychic Passenger](../Abilities/Psychic%20Passenger.md)
- Tier 6: [Mental Projection](../Abilities/Mental%20Projection.md) or [Improved
  Sensor](../Abilities/Improved%20Sensor.md)

GM Intrusions: Reuniting mind and body can sometimes be disorienting and require
a character to spend a few moments to get their bearings.
