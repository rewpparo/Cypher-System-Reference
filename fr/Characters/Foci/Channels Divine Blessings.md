Channels Divine Blessings #Focus #IrregularFocus #Core

A devout follower of a divine being, you channel some of your deity’s power to
achieve wonders.

- Tier 1: [Blessing of the Gods](../Abilities/Blessing%20of%20the%20Gods.md)
- Tier 2: [Enhanced Intellect](../Abilities/Enhanced%20Intellect.md)
- Tier 3: [Divine Radiance](../Abilities/Divine%20Radiance.md) or [Fire
  Bloom](../Abilities/Fire%20Bloom.md)
- Tier 4: [Overawe](../Abilities/Overawe.md)
- Tier 5: [Divine Intervention](../Abilities/Divine%20Intervention.md)
- Tier 6: [Divine Symbol](../Abilities/Divine%20Symbol.md) or [Summon
  Demon](../Abilities/Summon%20Demon.md)

GM Intrusions: A demon investigates divine magic use. A rival cult has issues
with the character’s teachings.
