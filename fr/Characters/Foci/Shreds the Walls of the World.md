Shreds the Walls of the World #Focus #MovementFocus #Core

Speed plus phasing gives you a unique ability to evade danger and simultaneously
inflict damage.

- Tier 1: [Phase Sprint](../Abilities/Phase%20Sprint.md)
- Tier 1: [Disrupting Touch](../Abilities/Disrupting%20Touch.md)
- Tier 2: [Scratch Existence](../Abilities/Scratch%20Existence.md)
- Tier 3: [Invisible Phasing](../Abilities/Invisible%20Phasing.md) or [Walk
  Through Walls](../Abilities/Walk%20Through%20Walls.md)
- Tier 4: [Phase Detonation](../Abilities/Phase%20Detonation.md)
- Tier 5: [Very Long Sprinting](../Abilities/Very%20Long%20Sprinting.md)
- Tier 6: [Shred Existence](../Abilities/Shred%20Existence.md) or [Untouchable
  While Moving](../Abilities/Untouchable%20While%20Moving.md)

GM Intrusions: Moving so quickly while sprinting sometimes leads to stumbles on
unexpected, exotic obstacles.
