Grows to Towering Heights #Focus #TankFocus #Core

For brief periods, you can grow larger and, with enough experience, to towering
heights.

- Tier 1: [Enlarge](../Abilities/Enlarge.md)
- Tier 1: [Freakishly Large](../Abilities/Freakishly%20Large.md)
- Tier 2: [Bigger](../Abilities/Bigger.md)
- Tier 2: [Advantages of Being
  Big](../Abilities/Advantages%20of%20Being%20Big.md)
- Tier 3: [Huge](../Abilities/Huge.md) or [Throw](../Abilities/Throw.md)
- Tier 4: [Grab](../Abilities/Grab.md)
- Tier 5: [Gargantuan](../Abilities/Gargantuan.md)
- Tier 6: [Colossal](../Abilities/Colossal.md) or [Lethal
  Damage](../Abilities/Lethal%20Damage.md)

GM Intrusions: Rapid growth knocks over furnishings or smashes through ceilings
or hanging lights. An enlarged character breaks through the floor.
