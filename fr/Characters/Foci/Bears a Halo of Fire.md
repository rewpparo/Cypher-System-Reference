Bears a Halo of Fire #Focus #EnergyFocus #Core

You can sheath your body in flames, which protects you and harms your foes.

- Tier 1: [Shroud of Flame](../Abilities/Shroud%20of%20Flame.md)
- Tier 2: [Hurl Flame](../Abilities/Hurl%20Flame.md)
- Tier 3: [Wings of Fire](../Abilities/Wings%20of%20Fire.md) or [Fiery Hand of
  Doom](../Abilities/Fiery%20Hand%20of%20Doom.md)
- Tier 4: [Flameblade](../Abilities/Flameblade.md)
- Tier 5: [Fire Tendrils](../Abilities/Fire%20Tendrils.md)
- Tier 6: [Fire Servant](../Abilities/Fire%20Servant.md) or [Inferno
  Trail](../Abilities/Inferno%20Trail.md)

GM Intrusions: Fire burns flammable material. Fire spreads out of control.
Primitive creatures fear fire and often attack what they fear.
