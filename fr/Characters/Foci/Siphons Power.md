Siphons Power #Focus #SupportFocus #Core

You suck power out of machines and creatures alike in order to empower yourself.

(Robots and other living machines should be treated as creatures, not machines,
for the purposes of siphoning power from them.)

- Tier 1: [Drain Machine](../Abilities/Drain%20Machine.md)
- Tier 2: [Drain Creature](../Abilities/Drain%20Creature.md)
- Tier 3: [Drain at a Distance](../Abilities/Drain%20at%20a%20Distance.md) or
  [Unraveling Consumption](../Abilities/Unraveling%20Consumption.md)
- Tier 4: [Store Energy](../Abilities/Store%20Energy.md)
- Tier 5: [Share the Power](../Abilities/Share%20the%20Power.md)
- Tier 6: [Explosive Release](../Abilities/Explosive%20Release.md) or [Sun
  Siphon](../Abilities/Sun%20Siphon.md)

GM Intrusions: Drained power also transmits something unwanted—compulsions,
afflictions, or alien thoughts. Siphoned power can overload the character,
causing feedback.
