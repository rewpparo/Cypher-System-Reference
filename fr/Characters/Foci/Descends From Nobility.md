Descends From Nobility #Focus #IrregularFocus #Core

A descendent of wealth and power, you carry a noble title and the abilities
granted by a privileged upbringing.

- Type Swap Option: [Retinue](../Abilities/Retinue.md)
- Tier 1: [Privileged Nobility](../Abilities/Privileged%20Nobility.md)
- Tier 2: [Trained Interlocutor](../Abilities/Trained%20Interlocutor.md)
- Tier 3: [Advanced Command](../Abilities/Advanced%20Command.md) or [Noble’s
  Courage](../Abilities/Noble’s%20Courage.md)
- Tier 4: [Expert Follower](../Abilities/Expert%20Follower.md)
- Tier 5: [Asserting Your
  Privilege](../Abilities/Asserting%20Your%20Privilege.md)
- Tier 6: [Able Assistance](../Abilities/Able%20Assistance.md) or [Mind of a
  Leader](../Abilities/Mind%20of%20a%20Leader.md)

GM Intrusions: Debts incurred by a family are owed by the character. A long-lost
sibling seeks to disinherit rivals. An assassin finds the character.
