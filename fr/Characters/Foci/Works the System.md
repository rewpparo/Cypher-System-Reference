Works the System #Focus #InfluenceFocus #Core

You can exploit flaws in artificial systems, including but not limited to
computer code.

- Tier 1: [Hack the Impossible](../Abilities/Hack%20the%20Impossible.md)
- Tier 1: [Computer Programming](../Abilities/Computer%20Programming.md)
- Tier 2: [Connected](../Abilities/Connected.md)
- Tier 3: [Confidence Artist](../Abilities/Confidence%20Artist.md) or [Skill
  With Attacks](../Abilities/Skill%20With%20Attacks.md)
- Tier 4: [Confuse Enemy](../Abilities/Confuse%20Enemy.md)
- Tier 5: [Work the Friendship](../Abilities/Work%20the%20Friendship.md)
- Tier 6: [Call in Favor](../Abilities/Call%20in%20Favor.md) or [Greater
  Enhanced Potential](../Abilities/Greater%20Enhanced%20Potential.md)

GM Intrusions: Contacts sometimes have ulterior motives. Devices sometimes have
failsafes or even traps.
