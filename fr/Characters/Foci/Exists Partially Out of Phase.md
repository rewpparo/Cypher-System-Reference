Exists Partially Out of Phase #Focus #MovementFocus #Core

A bit translucent, you’re slightly out of phase and can move through solid
objects.

- Tier 1: [Walk Through Walls](../Abilities/Walk%20Through%20Walls.md)
- Tier 2: [Defensive Phasing](../Abilities/Defensive%20Phasing.md)
- Tier 3: [Phased Attack](../Abilities/Phased%20Attack.md) or [Phase
  Door](../Abilities/Phase%20Door.md)
- Tier 4: [Ghost](../Abilities/Ghost.md)
- Tier 5: [Untouchable](../Abilities/Untouchable.md)
- Tier 6: [Enhanced Phased Attack](../Abilities/Enhanced%20Phased%20Attack.md)
  or [Phase Foe](../Abilities/Phase%20Foe.md)

GM Intrusions: The character is sent phasing into an unexpected dimension. The
character becomes lost in a large solid.
