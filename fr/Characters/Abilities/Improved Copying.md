Improved Copying #Ability #Core

You can use [Copy Power](Copy%20Power.md) to copy more powerful abilities. In
addition to the normal options for using Effort with [Copy
Power](Copy%20Power.md), if you apply one level of Effort, the GM chooses a
mid-tier ability that most closely resembles that power (instead of a low-tier
ability).

When you use Improved Copying, a copied ability must be low, medium, or high
tier according to how it is listed in the ability categories. It doesn’t matter
if a type or focus makes it available at a lower or higher tier.

Facilitateur.
