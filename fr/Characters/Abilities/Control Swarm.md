Control Swarm (2 Intellect points) #Ability #CompanionAbility #Low #Core

Your swarm creatures from your [Influence Swarm](Influence%20Swarm.md) ability
within short range do as you telepathically command for ten minutes. Even common
insects (level 0) in large enough numbers can swarm about a single creature and
hinder its tasks.
