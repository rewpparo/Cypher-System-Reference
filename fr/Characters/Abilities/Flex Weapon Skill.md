Flex Weapon Skill #Ability #Core

At the beginning of each day, choose one type of attack: light bashing, light
bladed, light ranged, medium bashing, medium bladed, medium ranged, heavy
bashing, heavy bladed, or heavy ranged. For the rest of that day, you are
trained in attacks using that type of weapon. You can’t use this ability with an
attack skill in which you’re already trained to become specialized.

Enabler.
