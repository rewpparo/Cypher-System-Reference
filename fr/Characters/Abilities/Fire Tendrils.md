Fire Tendrils (5 Intellect points) #Ability #SpecialAttackAbility #High #Core

When you wish it, your halo (from your [Shroud of Flame](Shroud%20of%20Flame.md)
ability) sprouts three tendrils of flame that last for up to ten minutes. As an
action, you can use the tendrils to attack, making a separate attack roll for
each. Each tendril inflicts 4 points of damage. Otherwise, the attacks function
as standard attacks. If you don’t use the tendrils to attack, they remain but do
nothing.

Enabler.
