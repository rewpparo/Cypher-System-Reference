Flameblade (4 Intellect points) #Ability #MetaAbility #Mid #Core

When you wish it, you extend your [Shroud of Flame](Shroud%20of%20Flame.md) to
cover a weapon you wield in flame for one hour. The flame ends if you stop
holding or carrying the weapon. While the flame lasts, the weapon inflicts 2
additional points of damage.

Facilitateur.
