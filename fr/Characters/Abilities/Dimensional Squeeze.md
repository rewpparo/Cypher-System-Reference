Dimensional Squeeze (2+ Intellect points) #Ability #Core

You cram yourself into a transitional dimension, allowing you to instantaneously
appear anywhere you choose within short range if you have a clear and
unobstructed path to that location. You can pass through an intervening barrier
if it has an open space that you could easily fit your head through—about 1
square foot (30 cm by 30 cm square). In addition to the normal options for using
Effort, you can choose to use Effort to pass through a smaller opening in a
barrier; each level of Effort used in this way reduces the minimum opening size
by one-fourth. You land safely when you use this ability.

Transitional dimension : A dimension where distances are shorter compared to
those in other dimensions, so travel through it is faster than normal movement.
