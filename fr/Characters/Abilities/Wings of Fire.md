Wings of Fire (4 Intellect points) #Ability #MovementAbility #Mid #Core

While your [Shroud of Flame](Shroud%20of%20Flame.md) is active, you can spread
wings of fire and can levitate, moving at a rate of up to 20 feet (6 m) per
round in any direction for one minute. You can also take one other non-movement
action on your turn.
