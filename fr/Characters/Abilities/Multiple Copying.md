Multiple Copying #Ability #Core

When you use [Copy Power](Copy%20Power.md), you can copy two of the creature’s
abilities at the same time. In addition to the normal options for using Effort
with [Copy Power](Copy%20Power.md), you can apply levels of Effort to copy
additional abilities, each level of Effort copying an additional ability beyond
the initial two (three for one level of Effort, four for two levels, and so on).

Enabler.
