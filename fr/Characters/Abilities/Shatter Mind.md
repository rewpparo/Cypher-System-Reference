Shatter Mind (7+ Intellect points) #Ability #SpecialAttackAbility #High #Core

Your words reverberate destructively in the brain of an intelligent level 1
target within short range that can hear and understand you. They destroy tissue,
memories, and personality, triggering a vegetative state. In addition to the
normal options for using Effort, you can choose to use Effort to increase the
maximum level of the target. Thus, to shatter the mind of a level 5 target (four
levels above the normal limit), you must apply four levels of Effort.

(The vegetative state created by Shatter Mind can be healed by advanced magic or
science, or by a condition remover cypher that cures psychosis.)
