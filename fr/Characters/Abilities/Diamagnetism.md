Diamagnétisme #Ability #EnvironmentAbility #High #Core

You magnetize any nonmetallic object within short range so that it can be
affected by your other magnetic powers. Thus, with [Move
Metal](Move%20Metal.md), you can move any object. With [Repel
Metal](Repel%20Metal.md), you are trained in all Speed defense tasks, regardless
of whether the incoming attack uses metal. And so on.
