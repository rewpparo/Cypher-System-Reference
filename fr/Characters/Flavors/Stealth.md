Stealth #Flavor #Core

Characters with the stealth flavor are good at sneaking around, infiltrating
places they don’t belong, and deceiving others. They use these abilities in a
variety of ways, including combat. An Explorer with stealth flavor might be a
thief, while a Warrior with stealth flavor might be an assassin. An Explorer
with stealth flavor in a superhero setting might be a crimefighter who stalks
the streets at night.
