Net #Item #Tool #WeaponLight #Moderate #Core #Fantasy 1gp

A net designed for battle rather than fishing. It has metal hooks at each
intersection to help catch your enemy. You can throw it with one hand. Action to
refold it so it can be thrown again. If you hit an opponent with the net, all of
their physical actions are hindered until they take an action to remove it. Can
be thrown up to short range.
