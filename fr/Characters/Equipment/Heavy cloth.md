Heavy cloth #Item #ArmorLight #Moderate #Core #Fantasy 3gp

Clothing that’s heavy enough to reduce the effect of attacks against you, such
as winter clothing or a fashionable leather outfit. Heavy cloth provides 1 Armor
against piercing or slashing attacks like arrows and swords, but not bashing
attacks like clubs or hammers. Heavy cloth doesn’t have a Speed Effort cost. It
can’t be worn with other kinds of armor.
