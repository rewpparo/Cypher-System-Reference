Sling #Item #WeaponLight #Inexpensive #Core #Fantasy 1sp

A small pouch connected to two cords. You put a stone or bullet (metal slug) in
the pouch, hold the end of the cords, spin it, and let go of one of the cords to
hurl the projectile. You can fire it with one hand. You need two hands to load
it. Action to reload. Short Range.
