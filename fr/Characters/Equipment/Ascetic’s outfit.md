Ascetic’s outfit #Item #Clothing #Moderate #Core #Fantasy 1gp

A simple outfit worn by monks and other people who eschew displays of wealth and
status. Specific styles vary by climate and the philosophical tenets of the
wearer, but a typical example is a loose shirt, loose breeches, sandals, a cap,
and several cloth straps that can serve as a belt, scarf, or simple adornments.
