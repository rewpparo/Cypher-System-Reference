Lantern #Item #Tool #Moderate #Core #Fantasy 5gp

An improved version of a lamp, with a wick that draws oil and glass or metal
panes to protect it from wind. A lantern creates normal light in an immediate
area and dim light out to the short area beyond that. It burns for three to four
hours on 1 pint (.5 L) of oil. If dropped, it is less likely to spill than a
lamp.
