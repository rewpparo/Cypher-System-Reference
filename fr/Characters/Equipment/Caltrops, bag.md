Bag of Caltrops #Item #Consumable #Moderate #Core #Fantasy 1gp

A bag of hard things you scatter on the ground to slow or injure anyone walking
through an area. One bag covers an immediate area and makes that area count as
difficult terrain. A creature can safely move through it as if it were difficult
terrain (half speed). If a creature moves through the area at normal speed, they
must make a difficulty 2 Speed defense roll or take 2 points of Speed damage
(ignores Armor).
