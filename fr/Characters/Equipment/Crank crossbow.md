Crank crossbow #Item #WeaponMedium #Expensive #Core #Fantasy 250gp

A weapon similar to a light crossbow, but it has a magazine that holds five
bolts. You turn a small crank to advance to the next bolt (this is not an
action). Action to load an empty magazine with five bolts, action to reload the
crossbow with a new magazine. It can be used as a rapid-fire weapon. Long Range.
