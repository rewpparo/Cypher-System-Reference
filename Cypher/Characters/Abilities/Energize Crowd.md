Energize Crowd (9 Might points) #Ability #ProtectionAbility #SupportAbility #High #Core 

You extend your [Absorb Kinetic Energy](Absorb%20Kinetic%20Energy.md) ability to up to thirty creatures within short range so that they also can absorb energy from physical attacks and impacts for one hour. If you have [Absorb Pure Energy](Absorb%20Pure%20Energy.md) or [Improved Absorb Kinetic Energy](Improved%20Absorb%20Kinetic%20Energy.md), these creatures can use those abilities as well. The creatures, however, cannot release excess energy as a blast. 

Action to initiate.
