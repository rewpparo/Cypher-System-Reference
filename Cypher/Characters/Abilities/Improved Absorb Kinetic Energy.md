Improved Absorb Kinetic Energy #Ability #MetaAbility #Mid #Core 

When you use [Absorb Kinetic Energy](Absorb%20Kinetic%20Energy.md), instead of being able to absorb 1 point of damage from a physical attack or impact, you can absorb 2 points. You can also store up to 2 points of energy from any source. However, you can still [Release Energy](Release%20Energy.md) only 1 point at a time. 

Enabler.
