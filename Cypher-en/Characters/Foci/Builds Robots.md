Builds Robots #Focus #AllyFocus #Core 

Your robotic creations do as they are commanded.

(The word “robot” is used in this focus, though the robot you create might look very different from one created by someone else, depending on the genre. Steampunk robots, organic robots, or even magical golems are all feasible “robots.”)

- Tier 1: [Robot Builder](../Abilities/Robot%20Builder.md)
- Tier 2: [Robot Control](../Abilities/Robot%20Control.md)
- Tier 3: [Expert Follower](../Abilities/Expert%20Follower.md) or [Skill With Defense](../Abilities/Skill%20With%20Defense.md)
- Tier 4: [Robot Upgrade](../Abilities/Robot%20Upgrade.md)
- Tier 5: [Robot Fleet](../Abilities/Robot%20Fleet.md)
- Tier 6: [Robot Evolution](../Abilities/Robot%20Evolution.md) or [Robot Upgrade](../Abilities/Robot%20Upgrade.md)

GM Intrusions: The robot is hacked, gains a mind of its own, or unexpectedly detonates.
