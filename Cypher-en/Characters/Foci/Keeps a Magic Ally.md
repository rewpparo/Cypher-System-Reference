Keeps a Magic Ally #Focus #Core 

An allied magic creature bound to an object (such as a minor djinn in a lamp, or a ghost in a pipe) is your friend, protector, and weapon.

- Tier 1: [Bound Magic Creature](../Abilities/Bound%20Magic%20Creature.md)
- Tier 2: [Object Bond](../Abilities/Object%20Bond.md)
- Tier 2: [Hidden Closet](../Abilities/Hidden%20Closet.md)
- Tier 3: [Minor Wish](../Abilities/Minor%20Wish.md) or [Mount](../Abilities/Mount.md)
- Tier 4: [Improved Object Bond](../Abilities/Improved%20Object%20Bond.md)
- Tier 5: [Moderate Wish](../Abilities/Moderate%20Wish.md)
- Tier 6: [Object Bond Mastery](../Abilities/Object%20Bond%20Mastery.md) or [Trust to Luck](../Abilities/Trust%20to%20Luck.md)

GM Intrusions: The creature unexpectedly disappears into its bound object. The bound object cracks. The creature disagrees and doesn’t do as asked. The creature says it’s leaving unless a task is performed for it.
