Focuses Mind Over Matter #Focus #EnvironmentFocus #Core 

You can telekinetically move objects with your mind without physically touching them.

- Tier 1: [Divert Attacks](../Abilities/Divert%20Attacks.md)
- Tier 2: [Telekinesis](../Abilities/Telekinesis.md)
- Tier 3: [Cloak of Opportunity](../Abilities/Cloak%20of%20Opportunity.md) or [Enhance Strength](../Abilities/Enhance%20Strength.md)
- Tier 4: [Apportation](../Abilities/Apportation.md)
- Tier 5: [Psychokinetic Attack](../Abilities/Psychokinetic%20Attack.md)
- Tier 6: [Improved Apportation](../Abilities/Improved%20Apportation.md) or [Reshape](../Abilities/Reshape.md)

GM Intrusions: One mental slip, and moving objects drop or fragile objects break. Sometimes the wrong item moves, falls, or breaks.
