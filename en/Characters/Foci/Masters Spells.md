Masters Spells #Focus #IrregularFocus #Core 

By specializing in spellcasting and keeping a spellbook, you can quickly cast spells of arcing lightning, rolling fire, creeping shadow, and summoning.

- Tier 1: [Arcane Flare](../Abilities/Arcane%20Flare.md)
- Tier 2: [Ray of Confusion](../Abilities/Ray%20of%20Confusion.md)
- Tier 3: [Fire Bloom](../Abilities/Fire%20Bloom.md) or [Summon Giant Spider](../Abilities/Summon%20Giant%20Spider.md)
- Tier 4: [Soul Interrogation](../Abilities/Soul%20Interrogation.md)
- Tier 5: [Granite Wall](../Abilities/Granite%20Wall.md)
- Tier 6: [Summon Demon](../Abilities/Summon%20Demon.md) or [Word of Death](../Abilities/Word%20of%20Death.md)

GM Intrusions: The spell goes wrong. The summoned creature turns on the caster. A rival spellcaster is drawn to the magic use.
