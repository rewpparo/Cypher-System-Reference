Howls at the Moon #Focus #TankFocus #Core 

For brief periods, you become a fearsome and powerful creature with control issues.

- Tier 1: [Beast Form](../Abilities/Beast%20Form.md)
- Tier 2: [Controlled Change](../Abilities/Controlled%20Change.md)
- Tier 3: [Bigger Beast Form](../Abilities/Bigger%20Beast%20Form.md) or [Greater Beast Form](../Abilities/Greater%20Beast%20Form.md)
- Tier 4: [Greater Controlled Change](../Abilities/Greater%20Controlled%20Change.md)
- Tier 5: [Enhanced Beast Form](../Abilities/Enhanced%20Beast%20Form.md)
- Tier 6: [Lethal Damage](../Abilities/Lethal%20Damage.md) or [Perfect Control](../Abilities/Perfect%20Control.md)

GM Intrusions: The change happens in an uncontrolled fashion. People are terrified of monsters.
