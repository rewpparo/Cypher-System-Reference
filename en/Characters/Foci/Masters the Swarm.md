Masters the Swarm #Focus #AllyFocus #Core 

Insects. Rats. Bats. Even birds. You master one type of small creature that obeys you.

- Tier 1: [Influence Swarm](../Abilities/Influence%20Swarm.md)
- Tier 2: [Control Swarm](../Abilities/Control%20Swarm.md)
- Tier 3: [Living Armor](../Abilities/Living%20Armor.md) or [Skill With Attacks](../Abilities/Skill%20With%20Attacks.md)
- Tier 4: [Call Swarm](../Abilities/Call%20Swarm.md)
- Tier 5: [Gain Unusual Companion](../Abilities/Gain%20Unusual%20Companion.md)
- Tier 6: [Deadly Swarm](../Abilities/Deadly%20Swarm.md) or [Skill With Defense](../Abilities/Skill%20With%20Defense.md)

GM Intrusions: A command is misunderstood. Control is erratic or is lost. Bites and stings are not uncommon for masters of the swarm.
