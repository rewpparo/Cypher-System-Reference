Camera Sight (3+ Intellect points) #Ability #Cyberpunk 

This ability has two different options.
- One, you become aware of all security cameras within long range.
- Two, you can hack a security camera that you are aware of that is within one mile (XX km), allowing you to see through the camera’s vantage point for up to 10 minutes. /  If you activate this ability and apply a level of Effort, you can gain the benefit of both options at once. 

Action to initiate.
