AI Assistant (2 Intellect points)

While in cyberspace, you summon an AI assistant that appears at any point you can see within a short range. The AI is a level 3 NPC with 15 health. The AI obeys your commands and does as you direct it. The AI remains until you dismiss it as an action or until it is killed. If the AI disappears because it was killed, you take 4 points of damage that ignore Armor and cannot summon the AI again until the next time you jack in. 

Action to initiate.
