Drone Split #Ability #Cyberpunk 

Your primary drone can split into two identical drones with all of the abilities of the primary drone. The split drones are each one level lower than the primary drone (so a level 4 primary drone becomes two level 3 drones). When they recombine, apply the damage from the more injured drone to the primary drone’s health. If one of the two drones is destroyed, you can repair the primary drone as described in Drone Buddy. 

Action to split or recombine.
